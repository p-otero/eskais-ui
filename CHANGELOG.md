# Changelog

Versions follow [semver](https://semver.org). Before 1.0, a minor version can break compatibility. Release with `bun pm version <patch|minor|major>`, which bumps `package.json` and tags `vX.Y.Z`.

## Unreleased

### Added

- `CommandItem href`: a command that goes to a page renders as a link (an anchor that keeps the item's `data-slot`, styles and `option` role) and follows the repo's fast-link rules: Enter, a click, its `shortcut` or a primary mouse press follow it, a `#/…` hash route by setting `location.hash` and a same-origin path with `location.assign`, while other `#…` anchors and modified clicks stay native. A link command never runs `Command onAction`, which stays for actions. `onFollow(href, { event })` routes on the client (and closes the palette), `fast={false}` waits for the click, and `target`, `rel` and `download` reach the anchor. `useFastLink` takes `anchorRole` for an anchor root with a role other than link. The docs site's search uses link commands, so a page opens in a new tab on Ctrl/Cmd+click.
- `shell`: `searchQuery`, `defaultSearchQuery` and `onSearchQueryChange` control the palette's search; `ShellSearch` takes them as `query`, `defaultQuery` and `onQueryChange`. Without them, every open of the palette starts empty.
- `shell`: `sidebarShortcut` (off by default), like `"mod+b"`, toggles the sidebar on wide screens and opens its drawer on narrow ones. It is the topbar trigger's `shortcut`, so the trigger carries `aria-keyshortcuts`, shows the keys in its tooltip and `ShortcutsDialog` lists it.
- `shell`: the sidebar's state is reachable. Pass your own `useSidebarState()` result as `sidebar` to read or toggle it outside the Shell, or call `useShellSidebar()` inside it (pages, `actions`, `brand`) for `collapsed`, `setCollapsed` and the `controller`.
- `shell`: a route's `mobile` takes `{ order }`, so the bottom bar's order needn't follow the sidebar's. Routes sort by `order`, then by their place in the list; `mobile: true` still works and counts as order 0. The bar still holds four.
- `shell`: a route's `placements`, a subset of `"sidebar"`, `"mobile"`, `"more"` and `"palette"` (all by default), says where it shows: `["sidebar"]` keeps it out of the bottom bar, More and the palette, and leaving out `"more"` or `"palette"` keeps it out of just that. `placements` decides over `mobile`: without `"mobile"` a route stays out of the bar even with `mobile` set, and listing `"mobile"` puts it in the bar as `mobile: true` does (`mobile: { order }` still orders it). Exported as `ShellPlacement`.
- `shell`: `sidebarHeader` goes above the sidebar's routes (the Sidebar's `header`), for a brand, a workspace switcher or a caption; `sidebarFooter` already went below. Hidden while the sidebar is a rail, under the title in the narrow drawer.
- `shell`: `main="fill"` gives `<main>` a definite height, the column left under the topbar (`--shell-column-height`) and, on narrow screens with a bottom bar, above it, so a page like `DocumentWorkspace` fills it with its default `h-full` and scrolls inside, without a wrapper sized by hand. A route's `main` overrides it while that route is current, so an editor can fill among pages that scroll. `flow` (the default) is unchanged. `node tooling/shell-check.mjs` measures it (`?demo=shell-block&main=fill`). Exported as `ShellMain`.
- `shell`: the palette's Go to commands are link commands (`CommandItem href`): each is an anchor to the route's `href`, so Ctrl/Cmd+click opens it in a new tab, and Enter, a click or a mouse press close the palette and go through `onNavigate` (or load the href, or set a `#/route` hash). `onCommand` runs only your `commandGroups`.
- `command`: `CommandFooter`, a line under the list for hints like the keys that move and close, in caption text over a top border.
- `shell`: `searchFooter` renders at the bottom of the palette in a `CommandFooter`, like `<Kbd>↑</Kbd><Kbd>↓</Kbd>` and your words for them. Nothing by default, and no copy of its own.
- `MasterDetail idleDetail="visible"`: on a container under 42rem, the detail stays under the list while nothing is open instead of hiding, for a live pane like a monitor. It sits at its content's height up to `--master-detail-idle-height` (50%), scrolls by keyboard, and its bar has a Collapse/Expand button (`labels.collapse`/`expand`, with a tooltip and `aria-expanded`) that folds it to the bar and keeps focus; with `collapsible` the idle detail gets the same button beside the list and folds to the rail. `emptyDetailTitle` titles the bar and names the pane. The collapsed state is the one `storageKey` remembers.
- `MasterDetail height="auto"`: sized by its content between `--master-detail-min-height` (16rem) and `--master-detail-max-height` (40rem), the panes scrolling inside, for a block in a page that scrolls. `fill` (default) still fills its parent.
- `url-router` (lib): `UrlRouter`, `{ read, navigate, href, subscribe }` over the app's location as `{ path, params }`, for blocks that keep state in the URL. `hashRouter()` changes a hash route's query without touching its path (`#/devices?device=a1`) and builds `#/path?query` hrefs; `historyRouter()` does it for the path and query string; `memoryRouter()` is for tests; `withParam` sets one param. Both browser routers keep `history.state` and notify each other. `createUrlParam(name, { router })` and `routerUrlState(router)` (`url-state`) go through one, so an app wraps its own router's API instead of letting a block write URLs the router rewrites. `UrlParamAdapter` gains an optional `href(value)`. Defaults are unchanged. A new guide, Routing and the URL, covers the adapters and controlled selection for routes of the app's own shape (`#/devices/<id>`).
- `SettingsPage` section links take their href from the `url` adapter's new `href`, so `createUrlParam("section", { router: hashRouter() })` links to `#/settings?section=id` and a new tab opens the route, not a bare `#id` that replaces it; without `url` they stay `#id` anchors, and `getHref` still overrides. `navigation="buttons"` renders scroll-only buttons (`aria-current="location"`, pressed by agents by name) for sections with no address.
- `ConfirmDialog triggerDisabled` and `triggerPending`: the trigger is disabled, or pending like `Button loading` (a spinner, `data-pending`, `aria-busy`, presses ignored while it keeps focus); while either is set the dialog refuses to open, so an agent's `setOpen` can't get around the trigger either. `SettingsDangerAction disabled pending` pass them on, and its other props (`data-testid`, `className`) go on its row.

- `MarkdownEditorHandle.insertText(text, { userEvent })`: the edit is a plain `"input"` as before unless `userEvent` says otherwise, and `"input.type"` counts as typing, so a `[[` or `/` inserted from a host button opens its menu. `MarkdownEditorHandle.openWikilinkSuggestions()` focuses the editor, types `[[` unless the caret already follows one and opens the wikilink listbox (through a `TriggerMenu` controller); it returns `false` and changes nothing without `suggestWikilinks` or when read-only.
- `MarkdownEditorHandle.runCommand` runs the slash menu's blocks too (`codeBlock`, `table`, `divider`), and `insertBlock(kind)` inserts one in place of the selection; `runCommand("table")` threw `RangeError: Unknown command`. The slash menu and the handle share one implementation (`insertBlock` in `markdown-editor-commands`), so a block lands the same way from both: after a blank line when text comes before it, with the caret in the fence, the first cell or after the rule. `MarkdownEditorCommand` now includes `MarkdownBlockKind`.
- `MarkdownEditor frontmatter="readonly"` protects a leading YAML block: an edit inside it or across its fences keeps only the part outside it (a CodeMirror change filter), and typing before the opening fence or after a fence that ends the document is dropped, so the note round-trips byte for byte. A new `value` still replaces the block. `initialCaret` (`"afterFrontmatter"` by default, or `"start"`) decides where the caret starts.
- `DocumentWorkspace documentId`: when the open document's id changes, the narrow layout's explorer drawer closes and focus goes to the document. The drawer closed only on an `a[href]` click inside it, so a tree of buttons, quick open or a wikilink left it open over the new note. `useDocumentWorkspace().closeExplorer()` closes the drawer from a control of your own (the wide column stays), and `explorerOpen`/`onExplorerOpenChange` control it as before. `node tooling/document-workspace-check.mjs` opens a note from a button in the drawer at 320px.
- `extractHeadings` gives each heading a `slug`, unique in the document (GitHub's rule, `-1`, `-2`… after a repeat), and `MarkdownView` renders the same slug on each heading as `data-slug`.
- `DocumentQuickOpen` searches on the server. `source` (a `PageSource<T, { q?: string }>`, as `ServerCombobox` takes) with `getItem` sends the query as `filter.q` once typing pauses (`debounce`), aborts the request a newer query replaces, keeps the server's matches and order and loads the next page at the end of the list, all through UI SDK's `useAsyncOptions` and only while the dialog is open. For a search of your own, `items` now go with `filter` (`false` to list them as they are), `onQueryChange` and `status`. Before, it filtered fixed `items` by their title, so a note that matched on its body was hidden. Loading and errors show in the list and are read out (`labels.loading`, `labels.error`, `labels.count`), and `labels.empty` can be a function of the query. The item depends on `@eskais/page-source`.

### Changed

- `DocumentWorkspace` is split into focused files, so no component goes over a 250-line component limit (`p-otero/max-component-lines` in a consumer's lint): it was 298 lines. `use-document-workspace-state.ts` holds the layout, mode and panel state; `document-workspace-center.tsx` the header, bar, document region and status bar; `document-workspace-panels.tsx` the resizable columns and the narrow drawers; `document-quick-open.tsx` `DocumentQuickOpen` (still exported from `document-workspace`). `npx shadcn add @eskais/document-workspace` writes the new files; nothing changes for code that imports from `@/components/ui/document-workspace`.
- The README, the docs site, the `eskais-ui` skill and `@eskais/mockup`'s hint pin the registry as `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/p-otero/eskais-ui/refs/tags/<tag>/public/r/{name}.json`. raw.githubusercontent.com resolves a bare `<tag>` to a branch of the same name first, and a stray `v0.2.1` branch served pre-release files under the documented `v0.2.1` URL; that branch is gone, and `tooling/check-ref-names.mjs` fails CI and the Pages deploy if a branch is ever named like a tag. The site's URL follows the released version.
- Items list `lucide-react` without a version, instead of the exact `lucide-react@1.68.0` that 0.2.1 ships, which wrote `"lucide-react": "1.68.0"` into a consumer's `package.json` and downgraded a newer install. shadcn 4.23 skips a versionless dependency the project already has and installs any spec with a version, so a range doesn't help either: npm rewrites `^1.68.0` to `^<latest>`, bun writes `^1.68.0` over an exact spec, and only pnpm keeps a newer install. Without a version, an existing `lucide-react` is left as it is with npm, pnpm and bun, and a new project gets the newest release (with pnpm's or bun's `minimumReleaseAge`, the newest old enough). The icons need 1.68.0 or newer, the exact version in the repo's `package.json`, whose age `check-dependency-age.mjs` checks. `node tooling/consumer-check.mjs` asserts the `lucide-react` spec in `package.json` and the installed version after every add, with npm and pnpm, and its `existing-lucide` scenario starts from 1.70.0.

### Fixed

- `DocumentOutline` in read mode revealed the heading at the entry's index among the rendered headings, so a filtered `headings` list (an empty `## ` left out) focused the wrong one. It finds the heading by its `slug` now, or by its text for a heading without one; `revealHeading` takes the heading (an index still works) and a mode switch keeps to the slug too.
- A note with frontmatter opened in `MarkdownEditor` put the caret before the opening `---`, so the first keystroke went into the YAML; the caret now starts on the line after the closing fence (`initialCaret="start"` keeps the old place).
- Ring `Meter` no longer overflows a narrow column: the `variant="ring"` root (`inline-flex w-fit`) grew to a long label's unwrapped width, so a `MeterLabel className="truncate"` pushed it past a 320px grid column. The root takes `max-w-full min-w-0` and `MeterLabel` `max-w-full min-w-0`, so a truncating label truncates; `node tooling/shell-check.mjs` measures it.
- `CommandItem href`: a Ctrl/Cmd/Shift/Alt+click on a link command no longer runs `Command onAction`. The click stays native (a new tab or window), and UI SDK ran the command's action with it, so a palette that closes in `onAction` closed behind the new tab.
- `command-dialog`: every open starts with an empty search, with focus in it. Inside a `CommandDialogContent`, `Command` controls its search and page stack and resets them in the render that opens the dialog (to `defaultQuery` and `defaultPages`), then highlights the first command through a command controller, so this holds when the closed content stays mounted (`keepMounted`, or an exit animation that never ends, as in some Electron windows). Nothing remounts, so the search field the dialog focuses is the one that stays: replacing it lost focus after a Ctrl+K reopen. A `query` or `pages` you control, a controlled `value` and `autoHighlight={false}` are left alone. Command items carry their value as `data-value`. `node tooling/shell-check.mjs` reopens a kept-mounted palette in Chrome (`?demo=palette-reopen-check`).
- `command-dialog`: one Escape closes the palette and returns focus to its trigger, whatever the search holds. UI SDK's Command clears the search on the first Escape and goes back a page on the next; `CommandDialog escapeKey="clear"` keeps that order. A `dismissible={false}` dialog leaves Escape to the search. The close reports `escape-key` to `onOpenChange`.
- `DataState` forwards the rest of its props (`data-testid`, `id`, `aria-label`…) to its root `div`, which keeps its own `data-slot`, `data-status` and `aria-busy`.

## 0.2.1 (2026-11-18)

### Added

- `locale` (lib): `useLocale(locale)` returns an explicit locale or the page's `<html lang>`, following it as it changes, and skips it on the server and the first render so hydration matches. `StatDelta`, `StatValue`, `QuantityValue`, `RelativeTime`, chart tooltips and data tables, attachment sizes, the inbox and the agent task board format with it, so a page in English no longer shows "12 %" on a machine set to Spanish. Each still takes `locale`; `Meter` and `Progress` already read the page's language through UI SDK.
- `settings-page` (block): `SettingsPage` lists its `SettingsSection`s in a navigation that sticks beside them on wide containers (48rem and up) and becomes a section picker (`Select`) that sticks on narrow ones, marking the section being read with an IntersectionObserver band below the sticky bars (`--settings-top`). Going to a section scrolls to it by id and focuses its heading without touching `location.hash`, so hash routers keep their route; `url={createUrlParam("section")}` records it and deep links and Back scroll to it, and `currentSection`/`onSectionChange` follow your own state. `SettingsRow label description` lays a control beside its label (a `Field`, so the label names it) and stacks when narrow; `SettingsFacts` is an inline `DescriptionList` for read-only facts; `SettingsDangerZone` edges its box in the destructive tone and `SettingsDangerAction` confirms through `ConfirmDialog` with `data-agent-confirm`.
- `dashboard-grid` (block): `DashboardGrid` lays a `stats` row (`DashboardStats` of `DashboardStat`, which draw a placeholder of the value's height while `loading`) over a grid of cards sized by its container: one column, two from 42rem, `columns` (3 by default) from 64rem, `gap-section` between cards. Rows are at least `--dashboard-row-height` (20rem) tall and the cards in a row share their height, so cards beside each other line up and none changes size between loading and loaded; a card whose content is taller grows its row rather than clipping it. `DashboardCard title description actions span rowSpan` is a region named by its title; with `status`, `data`, `error` and `onRetry` it loads through `DataState` (a skeleton, the error with Retry, `labels.emptyTitle` or `empty`). `DashboardChart` fills a card and takes its title as its name, `DashboardMeter label value hint` centres a ring meter in one, and `DashboardStat delta` takes a `StatDelta`. Labels are set once on the grid and overridden per card.
- `master-detail` (block): a list with a detail pane, used by any review queue, inspector or device list. `MasterDetail label renderList renderDetail emptyDetail header toolbar` lays the panes side by side and stacks them below 42rem of container width (a container query, so it works inside an app shell's main column): only the list, or only the open item with a Back button, never an overlay. The selection is controlled or uncontrolled (`selectedId`, `defaultSelectedId`, `onSelectedIdChange` with `cancel()`), and `url` keeps it in the URL through a `{ read, write, subscribe }` adapter: `createUrlParam(name, { in: "search" | "hash", history })` from the new `@/lib/url-param` works with plain, history and hash routers. Enter or a click opens a row; `followFocus` makes j/k move the open item; opening focuses the detail's heading and Close, Back or Escape (stacked) return focus to the row. `collapsible` folds the detail to a rail with an Expand button, `resizable` adds a named handle, and `storageKey` remembers both. Agents `open(id)` and `close()` it by its label. Every label is overridable.
- `List` virtualizes `ListItem` children. A row list whose children are all elements with a string `id` (a `ListItem`, or a row of your own that takes `id`) reads them as its data, as it does `items` with `renderItem`: past 200 rows (`virtualize="auto"`) only the rows in view render, j/k, Home/End and PageUp/PageDown reach every row and focus it, agents `select` rows that aren't rendered, and find in page reaches them; `title` is a row's text. Grouped lists, cards and other children render as before. Tests cover both paths with 5,000 rows.
- `StatDelta` (in `stat`): a change beside a Stat's value. `value` (a ratio by default, so 0.12 reads "12%"; `format` and `locale` change it) gives the direction by its sign, or pass `direction`; the direction gives the tone (up success, down destructive, flat neutral), `invert` swaps it for a metric where down is good, and `tone` overrides it. An arrow (lucide, mirrored in right to left) and the size sit in a soft tone chip, then your `comparison` ("vs. last week"). Screen readers hear `labels.up/down/flat(formatted)` ("up 12% vs. last week"), never the arrow or the colour.
- Ring meter: `Meter variant="ring"` with a `MeterRing size` child (`sm`, `md`, `lg`) draws the reading as a ring gauge with the value in the centre and the label below. It is UI SDK's `MeterTrack`, so the root keeps `role="meter"` and its value for screen readers; the drawing is `aria-hidden`, `tone` colours the arc, it fills the other way round in right to left, and it doesn't animate under reduced motion. `node tooling/shell-check.mjs` measures it (`?demo=gauge-check`).
- `description-list`: `DescriptionList` (a `dl`), `DescriptionItem` (the `div` HTML allows around a pair), `DescriptionTerm` and `DescriptionDetails`. `layout="stacked"` (default), `"inline"` (term beside details at `--description-term-width`, divided by rules, stacked again once the list itself is under 24rem, a container query) or `"grid"` (as many columns as fit at `--description-column-min`, at most `columns`). Spacing from the role tokens, so compact tightens it. An empty value shows a dash that reads `labels.empty` ("Not set"). `mono` for ids and hashes; `copyable` (with `copyValue`, `onCopy`) adds a copy button in the CodeBlock pattern: the value is a group named by its term with a `copy` action for agents, the button is hidden from them, and "Copied" is announced. `node tooling/shell-check.mjs` measures each layout wide and narrow.
- `shell` block: `Shell` turns one list of routes into an application's frame: a `SkipLink` to `<main id="main" tabIndex={-1}>`, a `Topbar` with the `SidebarTrigger`, a `brand`, the search triggers (`CommandDialogTrigger` with `mod+k`, `CommandDialogOpenButton` in `centerCompact`) and `actions`; a grouped `Sidebar` that sticks under the topbar, collapses (remembered under `storageKey`) and is a drawer on narrow screens; a `CommandDialog` with a Go to group of every route (`keywords`, the group's label) plus your `commandGroups` run by `onCommand`; and a `MobileNav` of up to four `mobile: true` routes and More, sticky at the end of the shell so the page always ends above it. `currentHref` (an href, or a matcher where the longest match wins) sets `aria-current="page"` in all three. Router-agnostic: `onNavigate(href, route)` for a history router, `#/route` hrefs for a hash router, or plain links that fast links follow; sidebar routes are always Links with `onFollow`, so agents follow them by name. Every string is a `labels` entry. A navigation re-renders only the two routes whose current state changed. `--shell-height` sizes it inside a pane.
- `SidebarGroup`: `label` is optional (a group without a heading), and `tone="muted"` quiets a group's items, like sections on their way out; the current page keeps its fill.
- `document-workspace` (block): a notes or docs workspace. Slots for an explorer, tabs, breadcrumb, toolbar, the document, a status bar, an aside (backlinks, properties, outline), an alert and quick open; `mode`/`onModeChange` (`edit`, `read`), `explorerOpen`, `asideOpen`, `saveState` (`saved`, `saving`, `unsaved`, `error`), `labels` and `storageKey` (the columns' state and widths). It lays out by its container (`narrow`, 760 px), so it fits an app shell's `<main>`: resizable, collapsible columns when wide, drawers when narrow. `DocumentQuickOpen` (a `CommandDialog`, Ctrl/Cmd+P through the shared shortcuts) and `DocumentOutline` (the caret in edit mode, focus in read mode). Switching modes keeps the reading position by heading; drawers, quick open and menus return focus to the document. Named landmarks and toggles that agents press by name. The docs page has a live demo with three notes, wikilinks, an embed, frontmatter, backlinks and a save-state simulator; `node tooling/document-workspace-check.mjs` drives it in Chrome (test:browser, test:compiler).
- `markdown-editor`: a `ref` gets a `MarkdownEditorHandle` once the editor mounts, so a host's Insert or Link menu and an outline drive it without importing CodeMirror: `focus`, `getValue`, `getSelection` and `setSelection(anchor, head?)` in offsets of the Markdown string (CRLF counted as written), `insertText`, `insertWikilink(target, alias?)`, `insertLink(href, text?)`, `wrapSelection(before, after?)`, `runCommand(name)` for the toolbar's formats and levels, `scrollToLine(line)` and `getScrollLine()`. `onWikilinkClick(target, event)` on Ctrl/Cmd+click or Enter in a wikilink or an embed. `embedPreview` (off by default) shows `![[target]]` off the caret as `renderEmbed(target, alias)` or a placeholder.
- `markdown-view`: `![[target]]` and `![[target|alias]]` embeds through `renderEmbed` (a link-like placeholder by default); `onWikilinkClick(target, event)` for wikilinks, which render through `Link` (fast links, hash routes, agents' `follow`) or as a button when they have no address; `frontmatter` (`"hide"` by default, `"properties"`, `"raw"`) and `renderFrontmatter` for a leading YAML block, read by `parseFrontmatter` with no YAML dependency; `extractHeadings(markdown)` for an outline.
- `page-source`: one backend-agnostic contract for paged data (`PageSource`, `PageRequest`, `PageResult`, capabilities for page or cursor positions and exact, estimated or no totals) and an adapter for each wire format, none of them tied to a backend library: `pagedSource` (0- or 1-based), `offsetSource`, `cursorSource` (opaque or keyset cursors), `relaySource`, `linkHeaderSource` (an RFC 8288 `Link` parser), `springPageSource` and `springSliceSource`, plus `arraySource` for demos and tests (latency, failures, abort). `createPageController` derives the request, goes back to the first page when the filter, sort or size changes, picks the navigation from what the source gives, debounces search, keeps the URL in sync and writes "Showing 21–40 of 312" for a live region. `readJson` throws an `HttpError` with the status.
- `filter-schema`: `defineFilters` declares typed filters (text, options, boolean, number, date, date range, remote options) that are also the field whitelist; `FilterValues` infers their values and `toFilterAst` builds one canonical filter. Serializers for flat query params (three naming styles), a JSON body, RSQL, OData, Spring Filter, Hasura or Prisma `where` and `q`-only search escape for their grammar and refuse what they can't write safely. `createUrlCodec` keeps schema values in readable, round-tripping params.
- `server-data`: `useServerPage`, `useServerTotal`, `useServerInfinite`, `useInvalidateSource`, `useSourceMutation` (invalidates by default, optimistic on request) and `usePageController` on TanStack Query 5, which never shows in their types. The previous page stays while the next loads, superseded requests are aborted, a 4xx is never retried. They use the app's `QueryClient`, or a shared default; `DataProvider` and `createDataClient` provide the defaults (two retries except on a 4xx, 30 s fresh, no refetch on focus).
- `DataTable manual`: the server sorts and pages. `rows` is the current page as given, sort and page changes are only reported, `total` or `pageCount` sets the pages, and columns sort by `sortField`. `busy` marks the table `aria-busy` while the rows stay, and `secondarySorts` shows each later sort's place on its header (`labels.sortRank`). Client mode is unchanged.
- `server-data-table`: `ServerDataTable`, a `DataTable` over a `PageSource`. The server filters, sorts (one column, or several with Shift+click under `sortMode="multi"`) and pages; navigation follows the source: page numbers with an exact total, previous and next without, load more or infinite for a cursor, with a page size select. `DataState` covers loading, error with retry and empty, the previous page's rows stay (`aria-busy`) while the next loads, a polite status says "Showing 21–40 of 312" after each load and "Loading…" only past 400 ms, and focus stays on the pressed control. `urlState` keeps filters, sort and page in readable params, with the hash or history adapters. Agents sort and page through the table and filter by label through its filters group.
- `filter-chips`: `FilterChips`, filters as chips in a `FilterBar`: one per active filter, opening an editor for its field (picks apply at once, typed values on Enter or close), a remove button that returns focus to Add filter, a menu of the other fields, and reset. `summarizeFilter` writes a chip's value.
- `filter-panel`: `FilterPanel` and `FilterPanelTrigger`, every filter in one form with Apply and Reset, in a `SidePanel` with `narrow="sheet"`: beside the results on wide screens, a bottom sheet at 760px and below. `ServerDataTable filterUi` takes `"chips"` and `"panel"`.
- `filter-field`: `FilterField`, the control for one schema field by kind (input, select, checkboxes, comparison and numbers, date pickers, `ServerCombobox`), named by its label. Every control has a visible label (From and To on a date range, Comparison and Value on a number, side by side in a wide container), and a remote option says Any until something is chosen. `ServerCombobox` reports the labels of chosen values with each change.
- `node tooling/consumer-check.mjs` has a `server-data` scenario: a fresh Vite app installs `server-data-table`, `server-list` and `server-combobox` with npm or pnpm, builds, and pages and filters them in Chrome.
- `server-combobox`: `ServerCombobox`, a Combobox over a `PageSource` (UI SDK `useAsyncOptions`, `filter={false}`): the server searches as you type, after a pause, aborting the request it replaces, and the next page loads at the end of the list, by page or cursor. `multiple` shows chips; `valueLabels` names values that haven't loaded.
- Server data formatting follows the page's language: the "Showing 21–40 of 312" counts, the page sizes, the combobox count and the chips' numbers and dates go through `useLocale` (`<html lang>`), with a `locale` prop on `ServerDataTable`, `ServerList`, `FilterChips`, `FilterPanel`, `FilterField` and `ServerCombobox` that wins. `formatCount` (`page-source`) caches one `Intl.NumberFormat` per locale.
- `server-list`: `ServerList`, a `List` over a `PageSource` that grows as you scroll (ListItem rows, virtualized past 200, row navigation), loading the next page when the end comes into view and with a visible Load more button that keeps focus. `useDelayedFlag` and `useReachEnd` (`server-data`) are its building blocks.
- `filter-search`: `FilterSearch`, the schema's search box (debounced, Enter commits, Escape clears, a placeholder from `FilterScope searchPlaceholder`: "Search {label}…" in a table or list), and `FilterScope`, which gives filter UIs their schema and controller and lets agents `filter("Status", ["Open"])` and `clearFilters()`. `parseFilterInput` and `findFilterKey` (`filter-schema`) read loose input into typed values.
- `url-state`: `historyUrlState`, `hashUrlState` (the query inside `#/route?…`) and `memoryUrlState` behind one `UrlStateAdapter` shape, and `useUrlState`.

- The task detail dialog is `xl` and sets a side column (meta, work, usage, links, location) beside the goal, acceptance and activity once it is 56rem wide; acceptance evidence folds into a disclosure and each attempt's usage toggle sits in its title row.
- A board column's collapse toggle moved to the end of its header with a fold icon and a tooltip, shown on hover or focus (always on touch and while collapsed).
- A thread with an `href` or `onOpenThread` opens from an "Open thread" button (`labels.openThread`) with its id in the tooltip; the bare id is never shown as text.
- `TaskStatusIcon` colours follow the column tones, so a proposal's icon is neutral, not warning.
- Dialogs and drawers fill with `--dialog-opacity`, so the task detail passes contrast under light glass.
- Fields share one surface style (`fieldSurfaceClassName`), and a `Field` holding a checkbox lays out as a row.

- Reduced motion stops infinite animations instead of making them flicker.
- `registry/lib/agent-task-board.ts` is now `agent-task-board-model.ts`, so it doesn't share a basename with `registry/ui/agent-task-board.tsx`. It also exports `activeAttempt` and `attemptCount`.
- Board labels gain `card`, `panel`, `table` and `filters.clearSearch`.
- The `theme` item ships the `dark` variant: `dark:` follows `.dark` and, before any script runs, the system scheme unless `<html>` opts into light. It stays below the one-line variant `shadcn` writes, which Tailwind then ignores.
- Installing `theme` into a stylesheet that `shadcn init` already wrote leaves no `--x: var(--x)` self-references in `@theme inline`: seeds, shapes, shadows and computed colours ship as `css` rules on `:root` and `.dark`. The radius scale runs to `4xl` and replaces the one `shadcn init` writes, and the base rules use the same `@apply` lists, so they merge instead of repeating.
- `theme` depends on `utils`: the theme's utilities (`text-meta`, `gap-items`, `rounded-surface`…) only merge correctly with its `cn`, which now also replaces `rounded-control`/`-choice`/`-surface`/`-floating` and `shadow-surface`/`-floating` with another radius or shadow.
- `text-caption`, `text-body-sm` and `text-body` no longer set a font weight, so `<strong>`, `<b>` and `<th>` keep theirs; meta, title and display steps still set one. `StageFilter` counts set `font-normal` to keep their look.
- `--link` and `--link-soft` derive from a new `--link-brand` seed (a blue, `oklch(0.55 0.2 260)`, by default) instead of `--brand`, so links keep a hue under the neutral palette. The colour palettes set it to their brand; the showcase theme editor edits it and adds it to the CSS snippet.
- The install docs list the `components.json` aliases shadcn needs to place registry files and rewrite their imports (`components`, `ui`, `utils`, `lib`, resolving through `paths` in the root `tsconfig.json`), and `consumer-check` installs through `~/` aliases.
- AGENTS.md and the skill say `--accent` is a surface only, never text or a stroke (use `border-input-strong`, `border-primary`, `ring-ring` or `text-link`); a unit test greps the registry for it.

### Fixed

- The skill's ⌘K recipe renders the `CommandDialog` beside the `Topbar`, with only its triggers in `center` and `centerCompact`: `center` is hidden on narrow screens, and a dialog inside it opened with no box. `tooling/shell-check.mjs` (part of `test:browser`) opens it at phone width.
- On narrow screens a `SidebarTrigger` opens its `Sidebar`'s drawer through the shared controller instead of rendering nothing, and a `Sidebar` with `trigger={false}` drops its in-place drawer button (`narrowTrigger` sets that alone). `<Topbar leading={<SidebarTrigger controller={…} />}>` is now one pattern for desktop and phone.
- A scrollable `TabsList` scrolls the selected or focused tab into view clear of the edge fades (by keyboard, pointer, an agent or on load, also inside `PageHeader`'s `tabs`), in either direction and without animating under reduced motion. It scrolls only the list, never the page.
- `CardHeader` (and `Section`) with an action is a wrapping row: the heading grows from an 11rem basis (`--card-heading-basis`), so a wide action wraps below the title as soon as the title would get narrower than that, instead of squeezing it to 8rem at a fixed 17rem container breakpoint. The heading parts sit in a `card-heading` wrapper; a `CardAction` must be a direct child.
- `Dialog`, `Drawer` and `ConfirmDialog` give focus back on close only from inside themselves, or from `<body>` while no other modal is open. A dialog that left the page while another modal opened (one whose focus moves in a frame later, like many third-party modals) pulled focus back to its opener behind that modal, which then closed. `useFocusReturn(finalFocus, ref)` takes the content's ref and returns `attach` for it.
- `MobileNav`: `--mobile-nav-offset` includes the bar's 1px top border, so a page's last line no longer hides under it. Labels never truncate: in a slot under 4.5rem they step down to the meta size, and they wrap to two short lines when they still don't fit ("Wellness" fits at 320px with five routes). The docs spell out the slots: up to five routes without `overflow`, four and More with it. `items` and `overflow` take readonly arrays.
- A `Form`'s `labelPlacement` reaches plain `Field`s inside it too, not only `FormField`s, unless the Field sets its own or holds a control that can't float its label. `FieldLabelPlacementDefault` (from `field`) provides that default.
- Readonly arrays are accepted wherever the registry takes options or items: `Select` `options` (and their groups) and `items`, `NativeSelect` `options`, `Combobox` `items` (now a thin wrapper over UI SDK's, whose type is mutable), `SiteHeader` `items`, `DataTable` `selection`, `DependencyChips` `items`, `AttemptTimeline` `attempts`, `StepForm`/`StepDialog` `steps` and `fields`, `UsageTable` `columns`, and `AgentTaskBoard` `tasks`, `columns` and `stages`. A type test covers each.
